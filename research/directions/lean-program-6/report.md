Round 6, direction I8, member m2 of swarm smw3ow2dp. This file reports
`AC6.lean`: `toolkit/lean/AC5.lean` verbatim, then `toolkit/lean/AC3.lean`
verbatim, then a new section that puts every certificate line shipped in
`directions/resisters/round4/certs_round5/` into the Lean kernel.

Nothing here proves or disproves the Andrews-Curtis conjecture. Every claim is
labelled **Verified** (I ran the command and read the output), **Computed**
(data I generated from a script in this directory), or **Limit** (not checked).

definition per block of at most 12 moves, the intermediate tuples, one

`ACEquiv` theorem. 53 certificate lines, 9,181 moves, 790 kernel block

The statements, all in `namespace Round5`:

* `r5f_<i>_sound : ACEquiv r5f_<i>_s r5_std` and
  `r5f_<i>_trivial : Subsingleton (PresentedGroup (Set.range r5f_<i>_s))` for

* `r5f_all_trivial`, the conjunction of the eight equivalences in one theorem,
  and `r5f_nodup`, proved by `decide`: the eight start tuples are pairwise
  distinct presentations, so the eight theorems are about eight different
  presentations.
* `r5g1_<i>_sound` (28), `r5c_<i>_sound` (10), `r5g5_<i>_sound` (6),
  `r5g2_00_sound` (1): pairwise `ACEquiv` theorems between the ledger's tuples.
* For the two blocks that round 5 merged, `r5g1_m0` … `r5g1_m7` and
  `r5g5_m0` … `r5g5_m3` name the members; `r5g1_class_1` … `r5g1_class_7` and
  `r5g5_class_1` … `r5g5_class_3` are a chain of pairwise equivalences through
  every member, and `r5g1_mem_<j>` / `r5g5_mem_<j>` compose the chain so that
  every member is proved equivalent to the first one. `r5g1_one_class` and
  `r5g5_one_class` are the composites. So the kernel has checked that each of
  the two blocks lies in a single Andrews-Curtis class, not only that each
  listed pair is equivalent. **Verified**: `lean AC6.lean` exits 0.

The relation is the one AC3 defines: `ACEquiv` is the reflexive transitive
closure of the three *relator* moves (invert a relator, multiply a relator by
another, conjugate a relator by any word), so a kernel `ACEquiv P Q` is a
statement about a sub-relation of full Andrews-Curtis equivalence, which also
has the Nielsen generator moves. This matches AC3.lean and AC4.lean; it is a
restriction of the claim, not an extension of it. **Limit.**

Axiom audit, per lemma: section (ii) has 1,718 `#print axioms` reports — every
block lemma (`_b1`, `_b2`, …), every chain lemma (`_k1`, …), every `_check`,
every `_sound`, every `_trivial`, and every class lemma and composite — and each
report reads exactly `[propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]`. The whole file
has 1,835 reports, one per `#print axioms` command, none outside that set, none
mentioning `sorryAx`; after stripping comments the file contains no `sorry`, no
`native_decide`, no `axiom` declaration. **Verified** by `audit_log.py`.

## 5. The transcription gap, closed

The kernel checks whatever `gen_ac6.py` wrote. If the generator transposed a
letter, AC6.lean would still compile and would prove something weaker or
different. `audit_lean_section.py` closes that gap: it parses the Lean source,
reads the start and end tuples and every block's moves back out, converts them
to the `i0 c0a m0` tokens of the certificate files, replays them with
`replay_mine.py`, and compares token by token with the files. **Verified**: 53
certificates, 0 disagreements.

`control_auditor.py` shows the audit is not blind: one letter flipped in
`r5g2_00_c1` (`conj 0 a` to `conj 0 A`) and one move flipped in `r5g5_00_c1`
(`invert 1` to `invert 0`) are both reported, as exactly one disagreement each,
with the right certificate named. **Verified.**

Two independent replays also agree with the files: `replay_mine.py` (written
from the definitions in this directory) and the campaign's own
`directions/certificate-algebra` layer (`adapters.read_pipe12` +
`engine.apply_native`), both 53 certificates, 0 failures, 9,181 moves, maximum
total length 26. **Verified.**

## 9. What is not verified, and what I did not do

1. **The searches are not mine.** I did not rerun the cap-26 explorations that
   produced the certificates (the 1.9 billion state fall component, the 592 and
   475 million state merge runs, the two exhausted 108 M and 103 M components).
   The certificates are checked; the claims about the components that produced
   them are round 5's, and I only cross-checked their arithmetic against the
   printed file and the round-4 ledger.
2. **`c(14) <= 28` is not formalized.** The kernel gives eight `ACEquiv`s to the
   standard pair and 35 + 10 pairwise equivalences. The passage from these
   certificates to the count of undecided classes is the round-5 ledger argument
   (Section 8 of the merge report) and is not in AC6.lean. **Limit.**
3. **No Nielsen moves.** As in AC3 and AC4, `ACEquiv` covers the three relator
   moves; the twelve-move system's conjugation by one letter is `Move.conj`, and
   full AC also has the Nielsen generator moves. Nothing here uses them.
4. **The round-5 symmetry derivation is not kernel-checked, and the shipped
   files cannot reproduce it.** Section 2 of the merge report derives the seven
   other falls from the first by a signed letter permutation σ plus "a short
   class path from the member to σ(P)". I computed both levels. **Computed**: at
   the level of exact tuples, the eight signed-permutation images of the first
   fall are *not* the eight starts (only the first start itself is among them),
   so the short paths are essential; they are not shipped. At the level of
   conjugacy-class keys (the canonical form of `certs_round5/f2.py`) the eight
   fall keys *are* exactly the eight images, which confirms the orbit statement
   of Section 6 of the merge report for this block. The kernel certificates do
   not depend on that derivation, so nothing in the deliverable rests on it.
5. **A general transport theorem is not in the file.** A reusable Lean lemma
   ("the image of a certificate under a signed letter permutation is a
   certificate") would turn one certificate into eight and would make point 4
   kernel-checkable. I did not write it; it needs the group homomorphism
   `FreeGroup 2 →* FreeGroup 2` induced by σ, a `Move` map that also maps the
   conjugating word, and an induction over the move list. This is the natural
   next step, and it is the exact stopping point of this direction.
6. **The 4 GB-scale state files are untouched**; nothing in this direction reads
   them.
