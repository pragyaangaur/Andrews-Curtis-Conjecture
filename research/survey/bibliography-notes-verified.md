# Working notes: sources seen live (D11 bibliography)

## Seen with full bibliographic record
1. Andrews, J. J.; Curtis, M. L. "Free Groups and Handlebodies." Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 16 (1965), no. 2, 192-195. DOI 10.1090/S0002-9939-1965-0173241-8. JSTOR 2033843. zbMATH 0131.38301.
2. Akbulut, S.; Kirby, R. "A potential smooth counterexample in dimension 4 to the Poincare conjecture, the Schoenflies conjecture, and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." Topology 24 (1985), no. 4, 375-390. DOI 10.1016/0040-9383(85)90010-2. MR0816520. Text: presentation {x, y | xyx = yxy, x^5 = y^4}.
3. Miller, C. F. III; Schupp, P. E. "Some presentations of the trivial group." Contemp. Math. 250 (1999). DOI 10.1090/conm/250/03848. Family <a,b | a^-1 b^n a = b^{n+1}, a = w>.
4. Bridson, M. R. "The complexity of balanced presentations and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." arXiv:1504.04187.
5. Zeeman, E. C. "On the dunce hat." Topology 2 (1963/64), 341-358. DOI 10.1016/0040-9383(63)90014-4.
6. Wright, P. "Group presentations and formal deformations." Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 208 (1975), 161-169. DOI 10.1090/S0002-9947-1975-0380813-5.
7. Gompf, R. E. "Killing the Akbulut-Kirby 4-sphere..." Topology 30 (1991), no. 1, 97-115. DOI 10.1016/0040-9383(91)90036-4. zbMATH 0715.57016.
8. Fox, R. H. "Free differential calculus. I." Ann. of Math. (2) 57 (1953), 547-560. DOI 10.2307/1969736.
9. Havas, G.; Ramsay, C. "Breadth-first search and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." IJAC 13 (2003), no. 1, 61-68. DOI 10.1142/S0218196703001365. Thm: 2-generator balanced, total relator length <= 13; if trivial then AC-equivalent to standard or to <x,y | x^3=y^4, xyx=yxy>; unique minimum potential counterexample.
10. Miasnikov, A. D. "Genetic algorithms and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." IJAC 9 (1999), no. 6, 671-686. DOI 10.1142/S0218196799000370. arXiv:math/0304306. All known balanced triv-group presentations of total relator length <= 12 satisfy AC; AK(2) killed with 21 moves.
11. Burns, R. G.; Macedonska, O. "Balanced presentations of the trivial group." Bull. London Math. Soc. 25 (1993), no. 6, 513-526. DOI 10.1112/blms/25.6.513.
12. Lisitsa, A. "New Andrews-Curtis trivializations for Miller-Schupp group presentations." Examples and Counterexamples 6 (2024), 100168. DOI 10.1016/j.exco.2024.100168. MS_n(w*) AC-trivial for n = 3..8.
13. Lisitsa, A. "Automated theorem proving reveals a lengthy Andrews-Curtis trivialization for a Miller-Schupp trivial group presentation." Examples and Counterexamples (2025), 100201. DOI 10.1016/j.exco.2025.100201. MS_9(w*), 8634 moves, Prover9, ~74 days, 56 GB.
14. Swan, J.; Ochoa, G.; Kendall, G.; Edjvet, M. "Fitness landscapes and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." IJAC 22 (2012), no. 2, 1250009. DOI 10.1142/S0218196711006753.
15. Panteleev, D.; Ushakov, A. "Conjugacy search problem and the Andrews-Curtis conjecture." Groups Complex. Cryptol. 11 (2019), no. 1, 43-60. DOI 10.1515/gcc-2019-2005.
16. Havas, G.; Ramsay, C. "Andrews-Curtis and Todd-Coxeter proof words." CUP (2003), 232-237. DOI 10.1017/CBO9780511542770.022.
17. Lishak, B. "Balanced finite presentations of the trivial group." J. Topol. Anal. 9 (2017), no. 2, 363-378. DOI 10.1142/S1793525317500182.
18. Fernandez, X. "Morse theory for group presentations." Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. (2023). DOI 10.1090/tran/8958.
19. Lisitsa, A. "The Andrews-Curtis conjecture, term rewriting and first-order proofs." LNCS (2018), 343-351. DOI 10.1007/978-3-319-96418-8_41.
